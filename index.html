SPAR Research Fellowship ’26

The Infrastructure of Perception

Governing AI Agent Behaviour Through Information Environment Design

Programme: SPAR (Supervised Program for Alignment Research) Completed: May 2026

Governance discussions about AI agents typically focus on two levers: access control (what agents can do) and alignment (what they want). This paper identifies a third, undertheorised lever: the design of agents' information environments. Three failure modes are analysed: externality blindness arising from inadequate success signals; reputation gaming in competitive agent marketplaces (supported by an RL simulation); and the emergence of agentic capitalism, in which personal agents become instruments of persuasion on behalf of third parties. The analysis argues for architectural governance of the shared information infrastructure through which agents perceive economic reality.
